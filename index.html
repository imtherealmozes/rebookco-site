Questions

Do you need my client list? No. We work inside your booking system as a staff user, and nothing is exported. That login can see client contact details, and you can remove it the moment we're done.


Who pays for the SMS? You do, directly to your booking system, and it isn't part of our fee. Marketing texts usually cost extra (Fresha, for example, lists 15c each), and they're billed when they send, whatever the result. We'll tell you the expected cost before anything goes out.


Will it work on my plan? It depends on the system. Some only include marketing campaigns on higher plans, and some can't filter exactly by last visit. Tell us your system and plan, and we'll say up front what's possible.


Is it legal? Yes. Messaging your own past clients is allowed under the Spam Act, as long as every message names your business and has an opt-out. We check how your booking system handles opt-outs before anything goes out.
